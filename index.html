Frequently
asked questions

01

How long does a typical video or VFX project take?

Timelines vary wildly based on scope. A 30-second motion graphic might take 2 weeks, while a live-action commercial with heavy CGI could take 6–10 weeks. We provide a detailed production schedule before any contract is signed.

02

Do you work with international clients or remote agencies?

Yes. We collaborate globally. Thanks to tools like Frame.io and secure cloud pipelines, reviewing edits and VFX shots is seamless regardless of your time zone.

03

Can we hire you just for post-production or VFX?

Absolutely. If you already have a director and raw footage, we can jump in as your dedicated post-house to handle editing, color grading, compositing, and final delivery.

04

What is your payment and milestone structure?

For project-based work, we typically require a 50% deposit to commence pre-production, 25% after the shoot or initial animatic, and the final 25% upon delivery of the master files.

05

What file formats and resolutions do you deliver?

We master in 4K by default but can deliver in 6K/8K or specific aspect ratios for social media (9:16, 1:1). Final deliverables usually include ProRes 422HQ for broadcast and optimized H.264/H.265 for web.
